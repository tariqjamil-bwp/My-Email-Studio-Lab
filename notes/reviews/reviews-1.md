**Hina R.** The new kitchen layout means two people can finally cook at once. The team walked me through every drawing before work began, and nothing was a surprise on the day.

**Faisal M.** The finished bathroom is lovely, with a considered layout and good storage. The work ran about three weeks past the date we were first given, and I had to chase for updates.

**Sadia & Omar K.** Every room now works together, and the flow from kitchen to lounge is exactly what we wanted. The final cost came in higher than the first estimate because of wiring problems found behind the walls, though it was explained clearly.

### 4. Kitchen and dining open-up: ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)

**Nadia S.** They noticed that the door swing blocked the vanity and fixed it on paper before anything was built. A thoughtful, functional result that feels bigger than it is.

### 6. Ground-floor remodel: ★★★★☆ (4/5)
**Tariq H.** Clear drawings, a tidy site and polite staff. I lost a star because the materials schedule changed twice and I only heard about it afterwards.
