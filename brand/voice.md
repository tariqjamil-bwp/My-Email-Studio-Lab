# Naqshar Architect Studio: brand voice

## Our voice in three words

Warm, clear, inspiring.

We write to homeowners planning a renovation. We sound like a knowledgeable friend who has done this many times: friendly, plain-spoken and encouraging, never stiff or salesy.

## Words we use

- home
- plan
- design
- thoughtful
- considered
- timeless
- functional
- lasting spaces
- your space
- sustainable
- innovative

## Words we avoid

- cheap
- quick fix
- fixed price
- best in town

## Claims we must never make

- A fixed price for any project. Estimates depend on the site, the scope and the materials.
- Any health or wellness claim, such as a detox, cure or healing benefit. We design homes and buildings, not health products.

## Claims from our website

These come from the Naqshar homepage. Keep them as written, and check each one before it goes into an email.

- Tagline: "Building Ideas Into Lasting Spaces."
- "Setting a benchmark for quality of spaces."
- "100% Satisfaction Guarantee."
- "Pixel Perfect Design": every detail is crafted with precision.
- "Quality Architect Design" and "Professional concept development."
- "Shaping cityscapes since 2020."
- Expertise percentages on the homepage: 82%, 86%, 89%, 90%, 92% and 94%. The page text doesn't clearly pair each figure with its label, so check them on the live site before using any of them.
