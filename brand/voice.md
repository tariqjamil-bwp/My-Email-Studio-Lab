# Naqshar Architect Studio: brand voice

## Our voice in three words

Warm, clear, inspiring.

We write to homeowners planning a renovation. We sound like a knowledgeable friend who has done this many times: friendly, plain-spoken and encouraging, never stiff or salesy.

## Words we use

- home
- plan
- design
- thoughtful
- considered
- timeless
- functional
- lasting spaces
- your space
- sustainable
- innovative

## Words we avoid

- cheap
- quick fix
- fixed price
- best in town

## Claims we must never make

- A fixed price for any project. Estimates depend on the site, the scope and the materials.
- Any health or wellness claim, such as a detox, cure or healing benefit. We design homes and buildings, not health products.
